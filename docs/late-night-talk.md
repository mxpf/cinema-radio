# Late Night Talk

An experimental station of Norm Nathan's WBZ broadcasts, drawn from the 20 oldest uploads to [Norm Nathan’s Vault of Silliness](https://www.youtube.com/@normnathansvaultofsilliness/videos), preserved and shared by producer Tony Nesbitt. These are episodes 1–19, including bonus episode 12A, in upload order. The years below refer to the original broadcasts.

Modern introductions and signoffs are trimmed; the original conversations, themes, station IDs, and advertisements remain. Cut points were reviewed against automatic captions and source descriptions, rather than a complete listening audit. The first recording starts at the listener-supplied 5:51. The [edit list](late-night-talk-edits.json) records every boundary.

This station is separate from the film collection and does not enter Repertory.

## Norm Nathan — Episode 01 (1995)

On July 15, 1995, Norm hosts the Dumb Birthday Game with callers from Massachusetts and Ohio, Jack Harte, and producer Tony Nesbitt joining by phone.

## Norm Nathan — Episode 02 (1995)

Across broadcasts from August 26–27, 1995, Norm interviews author Jean Aspen and takes calls about lottery dreams, Chinese food, and a mystery he wants listeners to solve.

## Norm Nathan — Episode 03 (1992)

First-time players tackle the Dumb Birthday Game on June 8, 1992. Norm and traffic reporter Russ Gannon add impressions, while commercial reads and late-night callers keep interrupting the business at hand.

[Broadcast source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cBqUVZciXxY)

## Norm Nathan — Episode 04 (1993)

The 1–2 a.m. hour of October 17, 1993 finds Norm chatting with callers, Sid Whittaker producing, and Fred Jendren delivering traffic reports. Original commercials remain part of the broadcast.

[Broadcast source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89iwfqwZtNA)

The conversation continues into the 2–3 a.m. hour on October 17, 1993, with callers from Boston, Quebec, and surrounding towns, plus familiar commercials and traffic reports.

## Norm Nathan — Episode 06 (1994)

The Swell Music Quiz on June 21, 1994 brings together Norm, Ed Mullen, Tony Nesbitt, and Jack Harte. Callers test their pop-music knowledge amid singing and studio banter.

## Norm Nathan — Episode 08 (1991)
