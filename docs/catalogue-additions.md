## Source basis

Each selected Internet Archive item explicitly carries a public-domain license or mark. Every title also appears in the [Desert Island Films public-domain catalogue](https://desertislandfilms.com/film-list/). This is the working U.S. public-domain listing basis used for the prototype, not an assertion of independent worldwide clearance. Selected files are older Archive transfers, not newly acquired commercial restorations. Exact filenames, source/derivative status, hashes, license links and technical measurements are in the manifest.

All are English-language films. Runtime differences between editions may reflect transfer speed or source cuts: Nothing Sacred is approximately 74 minutes and The Stranger approximately 91 minutes in these copies. We keep the full selected files without editing or speed correction; scene-by-scene completeness and a full human listening review have not been performed.

## Audio checks

Every output was decoded from beginning to end with no reported decoder errors. Output durations match their source files within two seconds. Each file uses Opus at 96 kbps VBR, with static gain toward -23 LUFS and a -2 dBFS sample limiter. No silence trimming, denoising, or extra dynamic compression. Untouched extracted source audio is retained in media/source-audio; temporary source video downloads can be removed after verification.
