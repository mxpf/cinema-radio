## Source and audio notes

Each selected Archive item explicitly carries a public-domain mark or dedication; every title is also listed in the [Desert Island Films catalogue](https://desertislandfilms.com/film-list/). This is the working U.S. public-domain listing basis for this local prototype, not independent worldwide clearance. Metadata, exact source URLs, upload dates, rights links, source hashes and audio measurements are recorded in `manifest.json`. No YouTube sources or recently labelled restoration uploads were used.

All outputs passed a full decode without reported decoder errors and match the selected source duration within two seconds. They use Opus at 96 kbps VBR, static gain toward -23 LUFS and a -2 dBFS sample limiter. No silence trimming, denoising, speed changes, or extra dynamic compression. Playback copies are in `media/audio/`; untouched extracted tracks remain in `media/source-audio/`. Temporary downloaded videos were removed after successful verification.

*The Flying Deuces* and *Borderline* had irregular source timestamps; their audio timestamps were rebuilt from sample count before encoding, without cutting or speeding up the sound.

These are full selected transfers; scene-by-scene completeness and full perceptual listening reviews have not been performed. The selected *The Man Who Cheated Himself* transfer explicitly notes crackle and missing frames in its old print. Runtime differences may reflect cuts or transfer speed.
