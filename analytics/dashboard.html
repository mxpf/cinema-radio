Visits are page openings counted once per browser tab per day, not unique people. Play starts count when audio begins advancing, including tuning to another station. Download clicks do not confirm installation.

Listening time excludes observed pauses, buffering, and seeks. It is approximate: closed windows, blocked requests, and background throttling can lose counts. Mac playback uses the same collector; the Android prototype is not measured.

No individual visitors are identified. Only daily aggregate counters are stored. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are respected.
